Question and choice of direction

Does earlier, correctly matched speaker History help identify an actually
recorded next utterance, beyond a shared six-turn current conversation?
We compared three paths before new responses: another generative S/F/H run
with an inadequately validated proxy; this bounded provenance-recognition
diagnostic; and a filtering algorithm without a semantically established
failure. The diagnostic had an objective, narrow target and explicit order
controls. It does not measure generated Persona Fidelity. See design_v1.json,
primary_evidence_v1.json and the prospective scientific_decision_v1.json.

The original S/F/H and Style-specificity64+64 studies remain frozen. Their
negative/limited conclusions are not repaired by this new task. The present
study is not a full factorial and NONE is not H=0: every arm has the same
six-turn current dialogue and final partner query. S numerical descriptors
and F explicit profiles are absent.

Materials and execution

Eight development speakers form four reciprocal pairs. Each person's actual
continuation is the other's alternative, with both A/B orders in each of
NONE, CORRECT and MISMATCH. Exact corpus provenance, not semantic superiority,
defines the target. Multiple continuations may be appropriate. All16 target
dialogues from the prior two generation studies were excluded;58 validation
speakers' target content was not deserialized. Both histories precede both
target dialogues; source/target partners differ. The selected materials use
24 disjoint participants across four pairs. History and full-prompt native
token differences are at most4, below the registered8-token ceiling.

Protocol success requires the exact valid letter identifying the actual
continuation. Invalid returned text is a protocol failure; its semantic
selection is undefined, not a known incorrect human interpretation. All main
responses are retained. Candidate invariance is reported only where both
orders give valid letters, hence18 of24 case-arm pairs are evaluable.

Forty of42 valid main selections choose A. Every evaluable reversal changes
the selected candidate text. A is always the first-displayed option, so the
experiment cannot separate an A-token preference from a first-position
preference. It establishes strong option-label/position dependence in this
configuration, not its internal causal mechanism or complete semantic neglect.

Decision and scientific boundaries

STOP expansion of this Qwen3-0.6B free-choice recognition configuration.
PIVOT interpretation to a measurement/reliability failure, not a History
benefit. The prospective deeper-investigation heuristic fails: incomplete
format coverage, zero CORRECT-NONE and a nonrobust CORRECT-MISMATCH contrast.
Do not tune prompts, aliases, thresholds or symbols against these cases, and
do not spend the58 validation speakers on this configuration.

This is useful negative developmental evidence: a narrow4/4 language/format
gate did not prevent pronounced selection dependence on real dialogue.
It does not establish factual/temporal reliability, generated speaker style,
general History harm, an independently validated mechanism, or a novel
algorithm. Stronger models could behave differently; no universal model gate
is inferred. Changing symbols or adding debiasing would be a separate study,
not a repair of this result and not inherently innovative.

The primary next path returns to generative measurement validity using the
already archived64 S/F/H outputs and genuine Japanese judgments. The existing
corrected review delivery remains the minimum human-only dependency; no new
ratings or mappings were manufactured. Review provenance must disclose prior
exposure; one researcher is not independent inter-rater reliability. Do not
run another weakly measured generative batch simply to maintain activity.
Algorithm implementation remains deferred until an interpretable semantic
failure and joint reliability/fidelity endpoints exist.

At this post-result allocation point, three feasible directions were compared
again. (A) Genuine review of the existing64 generated dialogues directly
addresses the project's unresolved construct-validity question with zero new
model calls, but needs a qualified human. (B) Crossing option symbols with
positions on new cases could separate two selection mechanisms; it is safe
and potentially informative but has strong existing prior-art overlap and
little immediate value for generative persona claims. (C) Repeating recognition
with a stronger model could test model dependence but would spend scarce
requests on another non-generative endpoint, without resolving semantic
fidelity. ChooseA as the primary critical path; do not runB orC solely because
they are executable. This is a resource-allocation decision, not a claim that
all legitimate future autonomous experiments are universally blocked.

Prior art and publication contribution

Response selection is established, not our invention: ACL2018P18-1205 and
ACL2020.acl-main.55 informed the provenance-versus-quality boundary before
execution. After observing the label/position pattern, targeted primary
reading found direct prior-art overlap in Zheng etal., ICLR2024, PriDe
(arXiv2309.03882v4; same work, not two publications), which examines ID-token
versus position bias and inference-time calibration. Generic debiasing or
option reversal cannot be claimed as an original PersonaSLM algorithm.

Ok and Lee, FindingsACL2026, compares context/question/option ordering and
uses architecture/intervention tests of context access. Its arXiv2601.14152

Our context precedes the options, so their context-after-options mechanism
