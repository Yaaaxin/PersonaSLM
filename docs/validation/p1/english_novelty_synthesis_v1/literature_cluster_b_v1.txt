PersonaSLM-specific scientific synthesis (our assessment, not paper claims)

The three intervention levels must not be conflated: an internal activation
intervention, selection between generated candidate continuations, and
parameter learning solve different problems and incur different costs.
None licenses a causal explanation for the already observed PersonaSLM Style
ratings. A lower resemblance score is not evidence that three particular heads
caused failure, and an EOS difference is not evidence for a confidence selector.

The information-access issue is decisive. Our natural current context, Facts,
and History can change what a defensible reply should contain. Removing those
sources to create a neutral candidate may remove necessary evidence. A future
selector must compare candidates with explicit, matched source access, and
validate source-supported behavior alongside genuine speaker resemblance.
Its budget must count both discarded candidates and selection work, not only
the delivered response. A confidence statistic must be tested against genuine
outcomes; confidence alone cannot stand in for correctness.

The available deployment path does not currently expose a verified per-head
intervention or exact competing-candidate probability interface. Building one
would require independently checked numerical behavior, a newly registered
configuration, fresh hardware bounds, and re-estimation of inference costs.
Training a 12B adaptation has no established safe resource plan here. These
are practical prerequisites, not proof that the published methods fail.
No new scorer/controller or model acquisition is justified merely by this
literature review.

The closest substantive overlap with real-person modeling is [8], but its
public-figure/name-memorization setting differs from anonymous professional
meeting speakers. The role-reasoning and synthetic-trait studies cannot
establish that the original S/F/H factorial question is already answered.
Conversely, that difference is only a possible empirical contribution scope,
not a proof of algorithmic novelty or first-ever factorial work. Broader
related-work synthesis and independently validated evidence remain necessary.

Recommended decision: GO on completing the unchanged mixed-model human
evaluation and its pre-join missingness/applicability analysis; then identify
whether a semantically meaningful configuration-dependent failure remains.
PIVOT away from an originality claim based merely on prompt switching,
candidate ranking, difference-in-means steering, or multi-objective persona
fine-tuning. STOP implementation of these interventions within this literature
task: the present 52 nonreserve generations do not justify uncalibrated
selection, broad hyperparameter searches, or weakening the 160-generation
validation reserve. If a future intervention is warranted, its minimum
credible comparisons include the unmodified model, a simple prompt/source
baseline, an existing relevant method, and cost-matched controls.
