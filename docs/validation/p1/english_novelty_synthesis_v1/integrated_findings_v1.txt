Question and decision

Which defensible contribution remains after comparison with close published
methods, and what does the existing English evidence actually establish?
Three paths were compared: (A) consolidate authentic-speaker factorial evidence;
(B) implement a reliability/fidelity algorithm; (C) collect another model/cohort.
A has the highest current information value: 300 archived responses and 99 real
human judgments already exist. B lacks a semantically established, technically
distinct repair target, and the closest steering, retrieval and ranking methods
are substantive prior art. C lacks a qualified independent English cohort;
spending the 52 nonreserved generations on another weak proxy comparison would
not resolve that limitation. None is chosen because it promises positive results.

GO: a narrowly scoped empirical manuscript with explicit incomplete RQs.
PIVOT: generic novelty claims become comparisons against known methods.
STOP: further inference based solely on native stops, repetition or latency.
This completes an integrated evidence-synthesis stage, not independent semantic
validation, the full research programme, or a submission-ready article.

Metric definitions:
- raw_empty: native content is exactly the empty string.
- whitespace_empty: native content contains only whitespace (including empty).
- repeat5_any: at least one repeated contiguous five-word window.
- repeat5_excess: (number of windows - number of unique windows)/windows.
- prefix20_repeat5_excess: the same ratio on exactly the first 20 extracted words.
Words use the existing case-folded English regex, not model tokenizer tokens.
Under five extracted words gives NA for full-response repetition; under 20 gives
NA for prefix20. NA is never zero. Punctuation-only or non-English content is not
silently classified as empty. No repetition threshold is a semantic quality gate.

Novelty classification (assessment, not a proof of exhaustive literature coverage)

KNOWN: Broad personalization/reliability or style/accuracy trade-offs. Direct
published precedents include Sun et al. ACL2026 and Ibrahim et al. Nature2026.
KNOWN: Generic relevance filtering, temporal semantic memory, draft-retrieve-revise,
persona/neutral candidate ranking, and additive persona activation steering.
These have direct precedents in the ten reviewed works and related methods.
PARTIALLY NOVEL candidate scope: jointly reporting genuine-speaker S/F/H source
provision, all ordinal threshold interactions, speaker heterogeneity and native
CPU cost in very small deployments. This is a candidate empirical gap under the
targeted review, not a demonstrated first, automatic novelty from smaller models, or an adequate
sample merely because no exact duplicate was found.
PARTIALLY NOVEL candidate evidence: the exact controlled configuration/case
results, including negative Style findings, applicability loss and replication
limits. Configuration-specific data alone need an important, validated argument
to support publication; repetition is not claimed as a new phenomenon.
POTENTIALLY NOVEL: no algorithm currently meets this standard. A precisely defined
real-speaker quality/cost mechanism could qualify only after genuine semantic
confirmation and stronger-baseline/ablation comparisons, none yet executed.
UNSUPPORTED: general speaker-specific fidelity gain, temporal contamination
causality, a pure size or instruction-training effect, calibrated population
significance, independent new-speaker replication, or a superior original method.
