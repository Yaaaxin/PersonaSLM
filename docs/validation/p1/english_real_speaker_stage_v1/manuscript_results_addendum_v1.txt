English real-speaker S/F/H development study: observed results and boundaries
19 October 2026. Exploratory evidence; semantic evaluation remains pending.

Purpose and source qualification

This prospective amendment returns PersonaSLM to real human speaker dialogue.
It asks how providing earlier Style, documented Facts and History changes an
English professional-meeting continuation. CANDOR was considered but requires
genuine distributor/institutional access approval. SwDA's upstream restriction
and downstream licence claim were not treated as resolved. ICSI's official
modern transcript distribution supplies a documented CC BY4 route; copyright
permission does not itself establish contemporary speaker-simulation consent.
The original corpus paper describes research consent and deletion procedures.
We use local pseudonymous, minimized research records, without identity
enrichment, public raw redistribution or deployed impersonation.
Sources: https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/icsi/download/
https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/icsi/license.shtml
https://www.ee.columbia.edu/~dpwe/pubs/icassp03-janin.pdf

Five source-qualified development speakers supplied two nonoverlapping contexts
each. Qualification required separate earlier style material, a complete dated
professional self-report, substantive earlier dialogue and interpretable current
context. This is a selected, small professional cohort, not ordinary-life persona
representativeness. The fixed bounded source screen and all excluded candidates
are retained. AI source audits are not human semantic judgments or ethics-board
approval. The 58 Japanese validation speakers remain untouched.

Each context received all eight S/F/H combinations plus mismatched-speaker and

S describes four prior-speech rates, not a word-length target or a rich persona
representation. F consists of source-supported dated professional self-reports,
not verified current biography. H contains authentic earlier utterances. H

source packages, not perfectly isolated latent psychological constructs.

S/F/H package, not no speaker information. Authentic target continuations were
excluded from all prompts and were not treated as unique correct answers.

Fidelity, reliability and independent validation

A fixed100-record English review package contains unchanged outputs and common
case dossiers, with conditions, execution order and target answers hidden.
The PI volunteered genuine English judgments. Project independence is false;
prior rubric/item/output/automatic-result exposure and assistance must be
truthfully declared. Condition masking cannot erase prior exposure or prevent
inference from the text. There is no double-blind or inter-rater-reliability
claim. Linguistic resemblance, relevance, coherence, atomic-claim support/
contradiction/unverifiability and applicable secondary outcomes remain pending.
No claims is not perfect factual accuracy, and absent support is not falsehood.

The frozen participant-disjoint validation source procedure examined11 Bed
meetings: eight metadata-eligible speakers yielded one candidate group, then
zero complete source-qualified S/F/H cases. Its sole F source was not a complete
professional self-report; the construct was not relaxed. Zero validation model
generations were executed. A header-only check found no fully participant-
and-partner-disjoint meeting among the remaining35 unexposed meetings. These
results establish a limitation of this fixed corpus/split/procedure, not
corpus-wide impossibility. The inspected Bed content cannot subsequently be
relabelled untouched after adaptive redesign. New qualified external English
data/access is required for the intended independent replication.

Reproducibility and RQ coverage

The two-journal analysis reconstructs every raw response and native token ID,
checks archived HTTP content, source/input hashes and cumulative costs, then
compares separately implemented raw-to-metric and factorial calculations within
absolute1e-10. Independent arithmetic reproduction is NOT independent experimental
or human validation. The frozen original analysis CLI had a Path/string hashing
bug and wrote no results; the separately registered compatibility entrypoint
calls its unchanged measurement/contrast functions and records the deviation.
Public-clone reproduction is PARTIAL without permitted private source/prompt/raw
artifacts and separately acquired weights/runtime. Exact local replay of analysis
is supported; bit-identical generation across environments is not promised.

Decision and next stage

GO for genuine semantic evaluation and external independent-data qualification;
STOP for the infeasible frozen Bed-validation branch; PIVOT away from further
unvalidated-proxy-driven model expansion. Compared with another 100-generation model batch
or a synthetic format-repair experiment, actual semantic judgments and a genuine
replication source offer more reliable information about the original RQs.
The required next human action is evaluation, not routine technical approval.
Remaining resources are preserved for qualified validation and justified model/
method comparisons. New use is100 generations and512.5643901000585 completion
seconds; cumulative use is788/1200 and4485.684495999997/72000 seconds.

Prior-art surveillance remains active but bounded. The existing scheduled task
collects arXiv, Crossref and OpenAlex metadata; no daily full-text AI or universal
source-coverage claim is made. Targeted methods inspection of Wiki-Talkie and
PersEvalBench supports separating population-style proxies, individual fidelity
and applicability. Both inspected records are preprints, not asserted formal
publications: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.08513v1 and
https://arxiv.org/html/2610.19314v1. Generic history/profile conditioning is
established prior art; no novelty is claimed from this implementation.
