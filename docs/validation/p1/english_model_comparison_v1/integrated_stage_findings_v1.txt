PersonaSLM: integrated English real-speaker developmental findings
2026-10-19 | Research Protocol v1.1 | P1 active, not research completion

Evidence level: DEVELOPMENT / EXPLORATORY. Genuine human evidence exists for
99 earlier responses. The newly completed 200 responses have independently
recomputed technical measurements, but their semantic evaluation is pending.
No independent new-speaker validation or algorithmic novelty is established.

1. Scientific conclusion and scope

This stage now contains 300 actual English real-speaker responses on the same
five development speakers and ten conversational contexts: the archived
100-response Qwen3-0.6B study and a newly completed, prospectively registered
200-response Qwen2.5-1.5B Base/Instruct deployment comparison. These are not
300 independent speakers, contexts, or human judgments.

The earlier genuine principal-researcher ratings provide a limited but
interpretable negative finding: supplying the registered Style representation
had negative paired ordinal net preference for relevance, coherence, and
perceived linguistic resemblance, under every allowed completion of the one
missing rating. This does not imply uniform ordinal distributional worsening:
the linguistic-resemblance contrast at rating >=4 is positive. Facts and
History effects also vary by outcome and ordinal cut. A blanket conclusion
that more persona material improves quality is unsupported.

The new experiment establishes that the same source packages have materially
different technical response and cost profiles across the two registered
deployments. Base generated 104.06 tokens per response on average; Instruct
generated 35.58. Base stopped at EOS in 16/100 responses, at the generation
limit in 73/100, and at a registered turn-boundary stop string in 11/100.
Instruct stopped at EOS in 98/100 and at the limit in 2/100. These observations
describe generation behavior, not semantic completion, fidelity, or truth.
Genuine review is necessary before deciding whether either configuration
improves dialogue quality or changes the earlier negative Style finding.

The main advance is an evidence-linked real-speaker factorial programme with
an actual human developmental finding and a completed matched-configuration
follow-up, not a validated new algorithm or a submission-ready study.

2. Why this experiment, rather than the main alternatives

The research decision compared three paths. History-selection baselines could
address RQ4, but the already permitted short-history pool did not support a
convincing length-controlled comparison between selection mechanisms. Expanding
that pool after seeing results would require new prospective source rules.
New-speaker transport was scientifically valuable, but prior English source
qualification had not yielded the required independent cohort; those failures
remain frozen. A single new Instruct configuration would cost less but omit a
same-family Base/Instruct comparison.

The chosen bounded pair therefore retained the original real-speaker S/F/H
question, matched all existing development contexts and controls, and tested
configuration dependence without additional source selection. It used 200 of
the then remaining 412 generations, leaving 212, including a 160-generation
independent-validation reserve. Selection was motivated by discriminating
information and feasibility, not an observed favorable model result.

3. Dataset, constructs, and experimental design

The source is authentic English ICSI professional meeting dialogue under the
previously verified CC BY 4.0 transcript distribution and attribution terms.
This qualifies a narrow local research use; public availability is not
individual consent to impersonation. Raw dialogue, actual speaker identifiers,
review mappings, and personal annotations remain private. The study does not
assert consent that was not documented, infer sensitive biographies, or deploy
speaker impersonation. Its subject is professional conversational behavior,
not a participant's complete personality or whole-life persona.

The same five development speakers each contribute two natural continuation
contexts. All eight C0-C7 conditions occur for each context; C0 has no added
S/F/H package and C7 has all three. C1 adds S, C2 F, C4 H, with the remaining
conditions combining those bits. M_STYLE replaces correct-speaker Style with
a mismatched-speaker representation at F=H=1. N_STYLE supplies the frozen
pooled-style representation at F=H=1. Each model therefore has 80 factorial
and 20 control responses, for 100 total. No reference continuation is included
in the generation prompt or treated as the only acceptable answer.

S supplies four development-source aggregate linguistic rates, rather than
private factual quotations or an exhaustive account of individual style.
F contains source-bound, dated professional self-reports, not independently
verified biographies or temporally verified world facts. H supplies actual
earlier conversational material. The current context is fixed across
conditions for a case. Earlier source chronology and prompt/reference
separation are preserved. Natural H inevitably contains linguistic style and
facts; C0 itself contains real conversational evidence. The estimands concern
provision of these source packages, not perfectly isolated psychological
constructs or differences under perfectly identical information access.

4. Existing genuine human findings: separate from the new model pair

The archived Qwen3-0.6B Q8_0 study has 99 genuine PI-authored primary judgments
and one UNAVAILABLE record among 100 allocated responses. The researcher
affirmed manual, condition-blinded evaluation and clarified the actual scale
as ordered 0-5, higher better. The original 0-3 rubric and its failed intake
validation remain unchanged. No rating was rescaled, repaired, or invented.
The ordinal analysis amendment was frozen after rating collection but before
the authorized condition join: this is retrospective exploratory analysis,
not prospective validation of a measurement instrument. Intermediate ordinal
categories were not supplied with invented verbal anchors.

For each main factor, 40 within-context pairs were allocated and 39 observed.
Net preference is P(higher rating with factor)-P(lower rating with factor),
averaged within speaker and equally across speakers. The following bounds
retain all five speakers and enumerate every possible 0-5 category for the
missing response. They are logical missing-outcome bounds, not confidence
intervals, imputed ratings, or population significance tests.

Outcome                   Style             Facts             History
Relevance                 [-.150,-.125]      [-.075,-.025]      [ .000, .050]
Coherence                 [-.100,-.075]      [-.050, .000]      [-.075,-.025]
Linguistic resemblance    [-.150,-.125]      [ .075, .125]      [-.025, .025]

All five cumulative ordinal cuts, rating >=1 through >=5, were analyzed for
all seven factorial contrasts; no attractive cut was selected as the result.
For Style and linguistic resemblance, the five contrast bounds are:
[-.075,-.050], [-.075,-.050], [-.050,-.025], [.025,.050], [-.075,-.050].
Thus the negative paired net is not first-order stochastic dominance. The
Style relevance profile is nonpositive at all five cuts, whereas coherence
has a different highest-cut profile. Facts can improve perceived linguistic
resemblance without improving relevance. History has no uniform benefit:
its relevance bound is nonnegative, coherence negative, and resemblance
crosses zero. The SF profile is nonnegative at all five cuts in the three
domains, but other interaction directions vary by cut. These dependent,
small-cohort profiles do not establish general synergistic mechanisms.

The three Style control summaries happen to be identical across the three
rated domains. Correct versus mismatched Style has 2 wins, 8 ties, 0 losses
(net +.20); correct versus pooled Style has 1 win, 9 ties, 0 losses (net +.10);
correct versus no Style at F=H=1 has 2 wins, 6 ties, 2 losses (net .00).
These are ten paired contexts per contrast, not three independent replications.
The zero net is not evidence of equivalence, and a small advantage over a
mismatched comparator is not proof of authentic behavioral Persona Fidelity.

Speaker heterogeneity is important. Among the four complete speakers, the
Style resemblance net values range from -.500 to +.125, with mean -.1875
and leave-one-speaker-out means [-.291667,-.083333]. Complete-speaker relevance
has the same mean but leave-one-out range [-.291667,-.041667]. These describe
a different subset from the full five-speaker missingness bounds. Leave-one-
out ranges measure influence, not sampling uncertainty or confidence.

The PI is not independent of the project. Prior absence of item exposure is
not verified; detailed annotation dates, aids, and competence declarations
are not invented. Double blindness and inter-rater reliability are not
established. Behavioral fidelity, factual/temporal correctness, applicability,
claim annotations, and confidence fields were not silently repaired or
analyzed as valid secondary endpoints. The conclusion concerns this assessor's
perceived relevance, coherence, and linguistic resemblance, not all constructs
of Persona Fidelity.

5. New executed Base/Instruct configurations and validity boundaries

Both frozen 100-request runs completed successfully, with 100 responses each,
no failed requests, no retries, no omitted outcomes, and no hidden generation
warmups. The Base run precedes Instruct; model blocks were not randomized.
Both use Qwen2.5-1.5B, static Q4_K_M third-party GGUF releases, the same native
CPU backend, 2048-token context, maximum 128 new tokens, four threads,
temperature 0, top_p 1, seed 42, and no prompt caching. Actual weight hashes,
metadata revisions, tokenizer IDs/merges, tensor layouts, prompt tokenization,
and runtime provenance are bound by the protocol, artifact receipt, manifests,
preflights, snapshots, journals, and raw HTTP evidence.

Base uses a plain conversational completion interface. Instruct renders the
same source material through its verified official chat template. Both use
the same case-specific participant/colleague turn-boundary stop strings.
The model-appropriate EOS definitions differ. Upstream GGUF conversion
provenance remains PARTIAL even though downloaded identities and layouts are
verified. Consequently this compares published checkpoint plus elicitation,
template, and stopping configurations. It does not isolate instruction tuning
causally. Both have the same nominal parameter scale, so it does not estimate
a model-size effect. The older Qwen3-0.6B run is a cross-family historical
reference, not a controlled size contrast.

The new configuration's 3-GiB prelaunch, 1-GiB server-ready and 512-MiB live
RAM gates were registered separately; no older threshold was lowered. The
prelaunch observations were 3,820,707,840 bytes for Base and 4,010,864,640 for
Instruct. Minimum recorded pre-request available RAM was 1,428,762,624 and
1,623,724,032 bytes respectively. Observed process-lifetime working-set peaks
were 2,515,021,824 bytes (2.342297 GiB) and 2,347,663,360 (2.186432 GiB).
These are run/process observations, not condition-specific RAM effects.

8. What the original research questions now support

RQ1, Style/Facts/History main and interaction effects: partially answered at
development level. The old genuine ordinal analysis establishes heterogeneous
PI-perceived effects, including negative Style paired net. The new experiment
establishes all seven technical contrasts in two same-family deployments.
New cross-configuration semantic effects are not yet established. Neither
body of evidence generalizes to the population of human speakers or all
possible representations of Style, Facts, or History.

RQ2, historical information and factual/temporal reliability: unanswered as a
claim of actual factual or temporal correctness. Professional self-report
provenance is not independent truth validation; the three primary ratings are
not substitutes for claim-level factual/temporal assessment. History cost and
old coherence/relevance profiles are relevant observations, not evidence of
temporal contamination causality. Unmeasured endpoints are not scored zero.

RQ3, model size and Base/Instruct effects: technical differences between two
registered same-family deployments are established. Pure instruction-training
effects, model-size effects, and semantic superiority are not. Old Qwen3-0.6B
and new Qwen2.5-1.5B data cannot identify size alone.

RQ4, retrieval, ranking, and LoRA quality-efficiency effects: not executed or
answered. There is no validated new algorithm, comparative algorithm advantage,
or controlled adaptation result. No LoRA training occurred in this stage.

Independent analysis reproduction is established; independent speaker
replication and independent human validation are not. The reserved Japanese
58-speaker set remains unused and is not substituted for an English cohort.
Earlier failed English source-qualification branches remain negative evidence,
not successful replications. Genuine replication needs a newly qualified
speaker/task cohort, frozen configuration and measurement, and adequate
resource authorization; computational reserve alone does not supply those
prerequisites.
