SCOPE AND STATUS

This closes a bounded computational analysis and replication-feasibility stage,
not the whole PersonaSLM programme or its semantic validation. It consolidates
100 previously executed real English model responses with a new raw-journal
native-timing decomposition. No new model inference was performed in this
continuation. Human-derived effects remain provisional; there is no submission-
readiness, independently replicated Persona Fidelity or novel algorithm claim.

DATA AND DESIGN ACTUALLY EXECUTED

Five ICSI professional-meeting speakers, two authentic conversational contexts
per speaker, eight S/F/H combinations and two matched-schema style controls:
80 factorial responses plus 20 controls. This is real-speaker development data,
not synthetic biographies. All 100 responses are present; native stopping was
EOS in every case, which does not establish semantic completion or correctness.

S supplies four rates of earlier linguistic behavior, not a full persona model.
F supplies dated professional self-reports, not verified current biographies.
H supplies earlier conversation. Common context already includes persona cues.
Sources differ in content and length; H can also convey facts and style. Thus
effects concern source provision, not perfectly isolated psychological traits.
Actual model: official Qwen3-0.6B Q8_0, revision
23749fefcc72300e3a2ad315e1317431b06b590a, llama.cpp b11573 CPU, four threads,
2048 context, 128 output-token ceiling, greedy temperature 0, seed 42. No prompt
cache, warmup or retry. Exact weights/runtime/tokenization/prompts are hash-bound
by the original protocol, manifests and separate original92/exact-resume8 runs.

NEW COMPUTATIONAL FINDING

Across all 100 responses, mean completion wall time was 5.125644 seconds:
4.194338 prefill, 0.844778 decoding and 0.086528 arithmetic residual. Prefill
accounted for 81.830455% of aggregate completion time. Native reused-input count
was zero for every response. The separate tokens_cached field is final slot
occupancy, not evidence of cache reuse.

The Style source reduced generated tokens but increased total measured latency.
History's +2.052547-second total contrast includes +1.915834 seconds of prefill.
Shorter answers therefore did not imply lower total cost in this configuration.
This is an arithmetic stage decomposition, not proof that semantic content,
history contamination or response length causally mediated an effect. Source
length and information were not manipulated independently.

UNCERTAINTY, DEPENDENCE AND INTERRUPTION

S/F/H total-time and prefill contrasts were positive in all five speakers.
Total-time speaker ranges: S [0.005786,0.755551], F [0.093911,0.427726],
H [1.219153,2.981658] seconds. Leave-one-speaker-out mean ranges were
S [0.441490,0.628931], F [0.214041,0.297494], H [1.820269,2.260896].
These are observed heterogeneity and influence ranges, NOT confidence intervals.
Five speakers share meetings, partners and topics; 100 responses are not 100
independent people. No calibrated confirmatory significance is claimed.

The original run stopped after92 between requests at the unchanged RAM guard;
a separate frozen exact-eight unattempted suffix completed the allocation.
This restart and non-independent execution order can confound timing effects.
Excluding the restart-affected speaker preserved positive main latency effects;
H was +2.260896 seconds and S +0.441490 seconds. The complementary context
exclusion changes context balance and the finite-set estimand. Neither check
recreates randomized load or eliminates all environmental confounding.

INDEPENDENT REPLICATION: BOUNDED NEGATIVE FEASIBILITY EVIDENCE

The original frozen ICSI Bed source procedure yielded no source-qualified cases;
zero independent-validation generations were performed. Remaining fully
participant-and-partner-disjoint ICSI meeting capacity under that procedure
was zero. This does not prove ICSI research universally impossible.

A separately frozen AMI natural-English source-qualification route used the
official manual annotation archive, documented CC BY4.0 distribution and privacy
limits. Roleplay/fictitious-scenario meetings were excluded. Metadata identified
26 natural meetings,51 stable within-corpus IDs and15 candidates with three or
more dates. The fixed earliest source/history/target schedule retrieved all
required source components for only3 candidates:11 lacked a retrieved first-
source self-report and1 had an unusable mandatory timed source segment. The
prospective minimum was4, so the conditional80-request study was NOT launched.
This is a fixed-procedure yield failure, not proof that AMI contains no suitable
facts. Subsequent narrow AI source curation is not genuine human evaluation.

No target-only meeting bodies were opened. Three future target meetings were
incidentally earlier sources for other candidates, affecting six candidates;
this exposure is recorded, not called untouched validation. Different corpus
pseudonyms cannot establish that actual people are disjoint across corpora.
The58 Japanese validation speakers remain protected and are not substituted
into the English primary programme. No validation data were tuned for a result.

RQ COVERAGE AND CURRENT SCIENTIFIC DECISION

RQ1: executed full source-provision factorial; technical main/interaction
estimates are reproducible. Semantic effects await documented genuine measures.
RQ2: dated evidence and authentic history are preserved; temporal correctness or
historical contamination effects are not established by these technical data.
RQ3: one model/configuration only; no size or Base/Instruct conclusion.
RQ4: measured prefill/decode costs constrain future fair comparisons; no tested
retrieval, ranking, LoRA or quality-preserving algorithm advantage is claimed.

Compared three next paths after the bounded AMI result:
A. Authentic-reference conditional NLL could measure predictability, not fidelity;
   a correct target-only native scorer is not currently validated. Fixed native
   perplexity options are not a ready exact target-span evaluation. HOLD rather
   than substitute an unvalidated endpoint or substantial new instrumentation.
B. ICSI Bro/Bns metadata permits a prospective new-target-speaker/known-partner
   transport candidate. Source quality is still unknown; target-speaker novelty
   is not full participant independence. Keep as a viable next empirical path,
   not an executed or prohibited study and not a repair of the Bed failure.
C. Consolidate the executed English experiment, actual native-cost observations
   and bounded source failures before creating more annotation debt. SELECTED.

GO for documented human-scale intake and registered, coverage-aware paired
semantic analysis when authentic definitions exist. STOP this fixed AMI80 branch.
PIVOT away from further proxy-only model expansion. New cohorts remain allowed
when independently justified; missing labels do not prohibit all other research.
The next primary empirical candidate is B after a new source policy, without
opening targets or changing the original study based on ratings. Larger model
and algorithm allocations remain held until a meaningful quality comparison is
available. Prefill dominance motivates a known efficiency baseline, not novelty.
