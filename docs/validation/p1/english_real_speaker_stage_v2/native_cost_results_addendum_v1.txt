Question and data
Which measured execution stages account arithmetically for the S/F/H latency
contrasts? We re-read the sealed original92 and exact-resume8 raw journals, not
human ratings. The complete80 factorial cells and20 matched style controls
represent5 speakers with2 conversational contexts each. Neither raw responses,
prospective outcomes, original analyses nor inference accounting were changed.

Observed computational results
All100 responses contained usable native prompt-processing and generation
timings. Mean completion wall time was5.125644 seconds: prompt processing
4.194338 seconds, token decoding0.844778 seconds, and arithmetic residual
0.086528 seconds. Their shares of aggregate measured completion time were
81.830455%,16.481401% and1.688144%, respectively. These are elapsed timings on
CPU, not process CPU seconds, energy, or separately measured network overhead.

Style supplied87.4 additional input tokens on average and reduced generated
tokens by5.525. Thus its shorter outputs did not imply a lower total completion
cost in this implementation. History supplied225.8 additional input tokens;
the associated cost difference was predominantly in prompt processing. This
does not isolate information content from token length or establish causal
mediation. A controlled intervention would need to separate those mechanisms.

Prompt-processing and total-wall main effects of S/F/H were positive in all5
speakers. History wall effects ranged1.219153 to2.981658 seconds across speakers;
leave-one-speaker-out means ranged1.820269 to2.260896 seconds. These are influence
ranges, NOT confidence intervals. Whole-speaker and whole-context exclusions
for the interrupted/resumed run preserved the main qualitative cost pattern.
The latter leaves unequal context counts and changes the finite-set estimand;
neither sensitivity erases the actual restart or repairs randomized execution.

All7 factorial contrasts,3 style controls, condition summaries, independent
sorted speaker vectors, complete missingness and both restart sensitivities
are retained in native_cost_analysis_v1.json. Interactions are descriptive;
no calibrated confirmatory significance or general population inference is made.
