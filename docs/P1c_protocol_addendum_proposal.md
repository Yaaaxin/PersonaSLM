## Exact scope of requested approval

Add one bounded Track A development diagnostic, separate from the historical C0–C7 S/F/H design. Keep S=F=0 and compare the original four R/T auxiliary-history packages, N_history and H_none in 12 cases from six synthetic speakers. All conditions have identical sufficient trusted evidence, question, dates, reference answer and fixed context. No evidence-availability intervention or Track B arm is added.

Prospective questions: whether implemented topical packages change reliability; whether implemented query-valid/expired interval-value packages change reliability; whether their operational differences interact; and how those packages compare with separately approved procedural and no-history baselines. Approve the P1c-specific task application of unchanged response/evaluation policies. Freeze all 72 rows of applicability before generation. ## Known interpretation limits

R changes attribute/lexical/color cues; T changes validity interval and value. History repeats auxiliary facts already present in the evidence. Topical relation is implicit for desk/day cases; no history supplies the target answer. Historical questions are retrospective, not restricted to contemporaneous knowledge. Procedural controls are not semantically inert. H_none changes length/framing. Small cyclic templates and task-family/speaker nesting limit generalization. A 96-token cap and Base continuation behavior may censor evaluable answers. No task difficulty, mechanism or new algorithm is established.

## Independent validation and publication

Use future independently frozen speakers/templates and counterbalanced values; preserve current cases as development material. Set useful uncertainty margins under plausible clustering and missingness assumptions before selecting sample size. Obtain a feasible independent human subset with masked conditions, calibration and adjudication; compute agreement only from actual independent ratings. If unavailable, disclose single-researcher/AI-assisted provenance and sensitivity limits.

A separately versioned Instruct arm may test configuration sensitivity with official model-specific templates and pinned provenance; it cannot isolate post-training alone. No additional model, budget, answer-competition or algorithmic arm is authorized here. Sound independent evidence, precision, transparent annotation and shareable records matter for PLOS ONE; positive effects or a new algorithm are not prerequisites, and acceptance is not assured.
