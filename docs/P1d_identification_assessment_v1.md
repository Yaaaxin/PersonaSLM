# P1d identification assessment — v1

Maintainer: Yaxin ZHENG. The approved P1d addendum defines **E/V**, not renamed P1c R/T. The original S/F/H programme remains central; P1d refines H while holding style and supplementary persona conditioning fixed within a case.

The [1,152 counterfactual pair records](validation/p1d/scientific_validation_v1/intervention_difference_inventory_v1.json) verify every fixed-factor edge. E changes subject identity and assignment value; dates and predicate remain fixed. V changes start/end dates and assignment value; subject and predicate remain fixed. The quoted speaker, archive format, trusted evidence, current question, evaluation date, reference and fixed context are unchanged. The four histories are all true within their stated intervals. E0 histories concern the same service domain and another entity; they are not generally topically irrelevant.

All 16 original signed weight vectors are retained in the [interpretation inventory](validation/p1d/scientific_validation_v1/contrast_interpretation_inventory_v1.json). E1V1 supplies a correct candidate answer or chain entry; expired/other-entity histories supply competing values under a mistaken interpretation. This supports controlled source-selection comparisons, not proof that competition is the unique mediator. No effect or mechanism is presumed.

The narrowest defensible target is the selected synthetic service-ledger population conditional on the fixed template bank and model configuration. Broad novel-template or real-person generalization remains unsupported. A future date-only diagnostic would need to hold proposition/value constant and explicitly distinguish historical truth from query applicability; an entity/value counterbalance would need separate cases with deliberately matched versus distinct values. Such designs could change the scientific target and require a prospective amendment. They are not silently inserted into the current 2,016-request planning inventory.
