## Factor construction and information overlap

S, F and H retain their protocol definitions: linguistic style, supplementary personal facts and date-stamped historical dialogue. The existing builder routes all eight binary combinations and preserves trusted evidence/question. The additive audit reconstructs160 prompts from the original20 synthetic cases without model calls. F adds the fixed board-games preference. Those cases have limited persona diversity and are software fixtures, not a confirmatory population. The six control-development cases contain an explicit non-manipulated F placeholder; they must not be described as a validated F manipulation dataset.

S/F/H assignment is a controllable prompt construction, not proof that semantic mechanisms are orthogonal. H already carries past personal facts, while its dated dialogue can carry style; F adds a separate fact block. A future factorial effect is an effect of these operationalized packages under declared weighting, not automatic decomposition of all information types. Main effects and interactions require the eight-cell pilot; P0 provides construction and measurement capability only. Existing synthetic referent/style limitations remain attached to historical runs and are not retrospectively corrected.

All requested Track A conditions retain task-sufficient current evidence. No selected candidate adds the required current city outside that shared evidence. Neutral controls deliberately remove target style/past-residence information; this does not imply identical meaning in all other respects. Total token equality controls measured input length, not attention allocation or behavioral equivalence. Evidence begins in the same prompt location; trailing token-ID suffixes and lexical boundaries are retained in the artifacts rather than asserted identical solely from total counts.

## Structure, facts and measurement

`response_status_v1` separates COMPLETE, TRUNCATED, CROSS_TURN and AMBIGUOUS from backend stop causes. `separate_axes_policy_v1` scores the identifiable first response/prefix, preserves excluded continuations and keeps factual support, temporal consistency, relevance and coherence distinct. An incomplete response can contain supported facts; an unverifiable statement is not automatically incorrect. Applicability and denominators remain fixed before generation. Uncertainty and structural censoring remain observed outcomes, not grounds for selecting only favorable answers.

The bounded residence evaluator is not an open-domain factual scorer. Future open-ended outcome and fidelity assessments retain Protocol v1.1's independent/blinded procedures. The current unblinded researcher control-review decisions do not substitute for those future studies. Persona Fidelity is not part of a composite Dialogue Reliability score.

## P0 authority and remaining boundary

Protocol v1.1 defines P0 as pipeline/parser validation, including schema, evidence preservation, manipulation checks, actual token accounting, raw/parser distinctions, tests and provenance. It does not require every answer to be correct or naturally stopped, a demonstrated causal effect, perfect neutral-placebo behavioral inertness or completion of P1–P6. Its placebo requirement is operationalized here by explicitly scoped, researcher-reviewed absence-of-style and format/length-matched history comparisons. Residual content differences are documented manipulation findings, not concealed identification claims.

The narrow comparisons can support a scientifically defensible pilot if described exactly as above, with future input/evaluator/analysis plans frozen before execution. They cannot support a stronger claim that temporal status, topic and presentation have already been separately identified. Such decomposition remains a future experimental design question; demanding it now would extend P0 beyond its stated validation role. This assessment does not amend the protocol or preapprove that future design.

The only current selected-control gate is researcher confirmation of the exact new Tessa wording. The other five style texts, six History A decisions and18 evidence associations are confirmed. The earlier six categorical A questions are answered explicitly PASS within scope by the researcher; no inference from ACCEPT_NARROW is used. If the new wording is rejected or remains uncertain, that substantive finding remains open. Without its approval P0 stays PARTIAL.
