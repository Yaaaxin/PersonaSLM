# P1c Identification Prior-Art Check — Review v1

Maintainer: Yaxin ZHENG. Primary-source methods were inspected for the scoped comparisons below. This is not an exhaustive novelty certification or an independent replication. Earlier [literature audit](P1c_related_work_novelty_audit.md) and [matrix](related_work_novelty_matrix.md) remain intact.

| Primary study and status | Verified overlap and methods | Remaining prospective difference / falsification requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Sun et al., **When Personalization Misleads: Understanding and Mitigating Hallucinations in Personalized LLMs**, Findings ACL 2026, DOI 10.18653/v1/2026.findings-acl.395 ([paper](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.395.pdf), §§3–5) | Personalization-related factual distortion; PFQABench; FPPS with offline layer/probe selection and inference-time steering. Reported factual gains can trade off personalized behavior. This is direct evidence-priority/persona-interference prior art. | P1c changes non-answer auxiliary records under a constant sufficient ledger. That is a narrower control question, not a new general interference finding. Independent package contrasts that vanish within useful bounds would narrow the proposed contribution. |
| Liu and Ding, **What to Admit and How to Present: Governing Persistent Memory in LLM Agents**, arXiv:2610.11188v1, 2026 **preprint** ([text](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.11188v1), §§3–5) | FC/PS admission policies and shared-verdict presentation comparisons; query-conditioned gating and matched-count random/relevance controls. Benefits are task-dependent and personalization tradeoffs remain; two rendering comparisons do not establish universal format equivalence. Conference formatting is not independently verified acceptance. | P1c does not implement admission and cannot claim that gating or rendering decomposition is original. A future comparison must preserve trusted evidence and test a specific residual beyond these baselines. No advantage on independent data would falsify a claimed method improvement. |
| Wu et al., **LongMemEval: Benchmarking Chat Assistants on Long-Term Interactive Memory**, ICLR 2025 ([conference](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/d813d324dbf0598bbdc9c8e79740ed01-Abstract-Conference.html), [v2 methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.10813v2), §§4–5) | Index/retrieve/read controls include time-aware query expansion, fact-augmented keys and Chain-of-Note/structured reading. Temporal memory and updates are evaluated; retrieving evidence does not ensure successful reading. | P1c supplies all required evidence in every cell; it tests additional ancillary conditioning rather than missing retrieval. A requested-date failure equally present without added history would favor ledger-reading explanations over interference. Date filtering is not a new algorithm. |
| Nakatsu and Wang, **Decoupling Logic from Persona: Structural Immunity of Edge LLM Agents to Context Pollution**, arXiv:2610.09772v2, 2026 **preprint** ([text](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.09772v2), §§4–6) | AO-DA separates logic/persona paths and includes a polluted-path ablation. Structural-format and raw-text scores differ; clean-path invariance follows constant inputs. Narrow tasks and configuration differences limit scope. | Our boundary/semantic separation cannot itself be claimed novel. Independently rated first-unit versus full-output condition differences could test measurement sensitivity; agreement within useful uncertainty would weaken a bias hypothesis. |
| Wallat, Nejdl and Sikdar, **When Facts Change: Temporal Knowledge Conflict Resolution in LLMs**, Findings ACL 2026, DOI 10.18653/v1/2026.findings-acl.103 ([paper](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.103.pdf), §§3–5) | Temporal knowledge conflict, stable/updated facts, true/counterfactual context and mutability prompting overlap prospective temporal claims. Detecting or verbalizing change need not guarantee the correct selection. | Current P1c uses historically true non-answer facts and explicit requested dates, not counterfactual target answers. Direct stale-answer competition needs its own approved design; do not infer it here. |

The listed methods/results were inspected in primary full-text sections, not inferred solely from titles. Complete appendices, source-code execution, asset licensing and reproduction were **not fully verified** in this task. A comprehensive answer-verifier survey also remains incomplete; originality for that direction is UNVERIFIED. The public web access ledger is retained with this review. No prior method is renamed as a new PersonaSLM algorithm.

Potential value lies in independently replicated, explicitly scoped conditional results and transparent measurement boundaries. Current P1c has no outcomes. The factorial layout, exact matching, small model and CPU deployment are not originality evidence. Presentation remains secondary; adding it would overlap direct admission/rendering research and increase the bank without resolving the present semantic question.
