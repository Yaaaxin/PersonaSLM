Maintainer: Yaxin ZHENG. Target: PLOS ONE. Official title: *Understanding the Effects of Persona Conditioning on Dialogue Reliability in Small Language Models*. This is prospective planning; no new model observation, established interaction or unprecedented contribution is claimed.

The [existing matrix](related_work_novelty_matrix.md) and completed Wave1 reports remain unchanged. Primary ACL/arXiv records were rechecked for the eight studies below; versioned HTML methods/results were available for the memory-admission and Wiki-Talkie studies. This is a scoped source comparison, not a systematic review, exhaustive priority search or reproduction of those experiments. ## Experiments that could add information

All hypotheses below are prospective and falsifiable. A near-zero estimate is a falsifier of a practically meaningful effect only when its interval is sufficiently informative under a margin approved in advance. An underpowered non-significant result remains inconclusive.

| Proposed experiment / question | Competing explanation and prior coverage | Additional evidence and possible falsifier | Necessity for first submission |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discriminative evidence-grounded calibration: does outcome variation emerge across source-verifiable task operations? | Wave1 may be easy rather than generally robust; contextual QA and memory reasoning are already studied | Demonstrates whether this instrument can observe errors/uncertainty without removing evidence. Continued ceiling or equally poor coverage falsifies its proposed utility; positive/negative persona effects are not required | Necessary for intended factual/temporal effect claims, or those claims must be narrowed |
| Independent S×H (with F controlled/averaged) on new cases | A total history effect or task/template imbalance can mimic interaction; persona sensitivity is well covered | Clustered, independently replicated paired interaction with stable interpretation after coverage/control checks. Informatively near-zero interaction or disappearance under valid realization controls weakens the claim | Conditional: necessary only if an interaction is a contribution claim; not every interaction must be pursued |
| Structural versus semantic measurement sensitivity | Apparent reliability differences may be continuation/parser artifacts; structural pollution already covered | A prospective comparison of frozen structural rates and separately judged first replies can show whether intervention conclusions change. Agreement of scoped estimates or only an enum defect weakens the proposed measurement contribution | Required measurement discipline; a stand-alone novelty claim needs replication, not just a high CROSS_TURN rate |
| R×T historical diagnostic | Relevance, current-answer reinforcement and dated competition are mixed in H; personalization/memory interference already studied | With carefully specified assertion balance, distinguish stale competition from relevance or reinforcement. Effects disappearing under those controls or a precise null falsify the scoped hypothesis | Optional until a clear hypothesis justifies it; do not expand the factorial automatically |
| Separate Instruct configuration on shared new cases | Task complexity, template and post-training may account for Base behavior; model sensitivity is established | Paired evidence on a scoped configuration boundary, retaining model/template confounds. No meaningful difference or full explanation by template weakens post-training interpretations | Claim-dependent, not a universal journal rule; defer until calibration |
| Independent materials / later authorized real speakers | Synthetic templates may determine the entire finding; Wiki-Talkie/TwinVoice cover naturalistic persona evaluation | Replication or a well-documented transfer failure provides an external-validity boundary | Independent evaluation material is necessary for broad empirical claims; authentic-speaker claims require later authorized data, not private data imported now |

Priority: validate measurement, then choose one defensible conditional-effect or informative-null question with independent confirmation. A larger generation count is not a contribution. If new tasks still yield no informative variation, retain that negative instrument result and reconsider the claim rather than manufacturing errors or selectively choosing persona-sensitive items.

## PLOS ONE readiness

PLOS ONE's [official publication criteria](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/criteria-for-publication) require technically sound research, appropriate analyses, supported conclusions and reporting/data standards, and consider negative/null results. They do not prescribe a universal number of models or guarantee acceptance of a factorial pilot. This design package is not a completed empirical Research Article or an automatically eligible protocol submission.

A defensible manuscript needs: a concrete question and prior-work contrast; independently validated stimuli and scoring; adequate clustered precision for the claims; a locked confirmatory analysis if confirmatory language is used; retained failures/nulls and honest missingness; and a reproducible release with genuine annotation provenance. The current12-speaker pilot and proposed24 prototypes are development evidence only. Original96 annotations remain single-researcher confirmed after assisted structured coding; no independent second annotator or agreement is invented.
