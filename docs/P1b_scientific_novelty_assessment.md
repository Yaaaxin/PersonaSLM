# P1b Scientific Novelty Assessment

Maintainer: Yaxin ZHENG. Paper: *Understanding the Effects of Persona Conditioning on Dialogue Reliability in Small Language Models*. Target: PLOS ONE. Protocol v1.1 remains formal; v1.2 Revised Proposed r1 is unadopted. Exploratory development evidence only. No new inference.

## Evidence, not an originality declaration

The record now establishes mixed categorical/task/ordinal patterns in this synthetic 2B Base calibration. It does not establish a universal persona-harm effect, a temporal mechanism or an original algorithm. Literature below was rechecked at official metadata/abstract scope in this task; no experiment, code or method was reproduced. The [existing matrix](related_work_novelty_matrix.md) remains intact.

## Candidate empirical contributions and falsifiers

| Candidate | Observed support / absence | Alternatives and missing evidence | Falsifiable next test / publication value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S/F/H interaction boundaries | Temporal SxH mean 0 with four opposing case contributions; task SxH negative but weak/sensitive | Small clusters, multiplicity, uncertainty and nested style/speaker; no general interaction | Independent counterbalanced speakers/templates. Narrow intervals near zero or reversed patterns would weaken a stable interaction hypothesis |
| Conditional reliability | A: 24/24 full; D: 24 Correct but 4 full; C/E: 28 contradictions | Families differ in wording, reference structure and relevance | Independently cross task operations and lexical realizations; disappearance after crossing would falsify a family mechanism |
| Temporal status under sufficient evidence | 34 adopted temporal contradictions;H total-package task difference | Four C0 errors; trusted ledger itself offers old values; no R/T isolation | Controlled reference-date/relevance diagnostic with unchanged trusted evidence and neutral baselines |
| Structural versus semantic measurement | 186 cross-turns include 109 full correct first replies; one ambiguous span changes a paired ordinal mean | Known format-failure literature; researcher-adopted AI codes, not independent reliability | Independent human unit/claim annotation plus frozen sensitivity rules; stable results under all defensible units would narrow the measurement-bias hypothesis |
| Model/external sensitivity | No comparison yet | Onlyone Base GGUF, synthetic speakers; fidelity not measured | Separately versioned native-template Instruct arm and later authorized real-speaker material; failure to replicate defines scope, not a universal law |


These are potential empirical contributions if replicated with defensible measurement and useful precision. Factorial design, CPU execution, model size and increased error counts are not originality evidence. Null/mixed results are retained; no new prior-art gap is claimed proven.
