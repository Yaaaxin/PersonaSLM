# P1c Related Work and Novelty Audit

Maintainer: Yaxin ZHENG. This is a scoped primary-source audit, not a systematic review or reproduction. Prospective contribution claims remain unverified. The search/access register is in [the proposal directory](validation/p1c/proposed_v1/).

## Search and verification scope

The search began with the researcher's seven direct competitors and followed primary links on ACL Anthology, arXiv and ICLR. Supplementary queries covered temporal evidence conflicts and irrelevant context. Secondary summaries and search-engine snippets were not used to establish experimental methods. Relevant full-text method/result sections were inspected; implementations, datasets and reported statistics were not independently reproduced. Bibliographic versions below delimit the claims. Missing implementation details are **NOT FULLY VERIFIED** rather than filled from memory.

## Implications for prospective contributions

| Candidate contribution | Actual support now | Competing explanation and needed evidence | Falsifiable next result / novelty risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conditional history reliability | P1b H package differences, including errors without H | Topic, answer repetition, length, dates and template are confounded | Independent scoped R/T contrasts with useful precision; disappearance after controls narrows claim. High prior-art overlap |
| Requested-date validity rather than newest-fact preference | P1b has dated errors; current proposal explicitly pairs current/historical questions | Ledger selection alone, lexical shortcuts, bounded output | Failure tied to valid/expired ancillary history after review versus equal no-history failures. Auxiliary cross cannot establish direct stale-answer competition |
| Structural/semantic measurement divergence | Preserved continuations and incomplete/incorrect first replies differ | Parser unit ambiguity and single-reviewer adoption | Independently rated units change or preserve paired semantic conclusions; no bias implies a useful null. Separation itself is already studied |
| Model/template sensitivity | One Base configuration only | Chat formatting, quantization and training lineage all change | A separately controlled arm reproduces or narrows within-arm contrasts. Size/deployment is not novelty |

Presentation should remain secondary: a controlled admission-versus-rendering study already exists. The potential contribution is a reproducible, scoped conditional pattern under fixed sufficient evidence and honest missingness, if independent data support it. No unprecedented method, empirical law or algorithm is established by this audit.
