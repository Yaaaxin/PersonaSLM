# PersonaSLM manuscript methods — working development text v1

Maintainer and intended paper author: Yaxin ZHENG.

Official title: Understanding the Effects of Persona Conditioning on Dialogue Reliability in Small Language Models. First submission target: PLOS ONE. This is working methods text, not a submitted manuscript or a claim of completed independent validation.

PersonaSLM examines whether and how linguistic style, supplementary personal facts and historical dialogue affect dialogue reliability when sufficient trusted evidence is held fixed. The research programme separates infrastructure validation (P0), S/F/H development exploration (P1a), demanding task and delivery calibration (P1b), historical-package calibration (P1c), prospective independent empirical validation (P1d), model/configuration sensitivity (P3), real-speaker external validity (P4), failure localization (P5), and algorithm development with ablations (P6). Completion of one development phase does not imply completion of later phases.

Within a paired case, conditions retain the same evidence, question, requested date, fixed context and answer requirements. P1d's E/V manipulations select historical statements by entity match and requested-interval match. They also change names, values and temporal expressions; conclusions must concern these text packages. The answer-supporting cell is an explicit positive control. Procedural controls match token length but are not established as semantically neutral; no-history prompts are shorter. A mathematical interaction does not identify a unique cognitive mechanism.

Evaluation separates factual claim support, temporal interpretation, relevance, coherence, complete task success, required components, semantic completeness, structural boundaries and narrow persona style fidelity. Incorrect claims, unsupported claims, unresolved attribution, unavailable boundaries and missing annotation are retained distinctly. Raw continuations and extracted first replies are preserved. AI-assisted proposals adopted by the researcher are reported as such; this provenance is not independent human annotation or inter-rater reliability. Independent review and adjudication must be reported only when actually obtained.

Analyses preserve paired cases and speaker clustering. Prospective primary comparisons, auxiliary exploratory contrasts, unknown-outcome bounds, boundary sensitivities and model-subset estimands are distinguished. Synthetic personas and shared templates constrain the sampling frame; bootstrap sensitivity is not a guarantee of population coverage. All adverse and null outcomes remain in the record. Experimental requests, prompts, tokens, model/backend files, software, randomization, raw responses and measurement versions are bound by hashes and immutable logs. Historical Protocol v1.1 is preserved; proposed documents are adopted only through explicit registered decisions.

The present working text deliberately contains no future P1d validation results, fabricated independent annotation, acceptance prediction or claim of algorithmic originality. Final model allocation, measurement readiness and output policy must follow their actual prospective freezes.
