Conference status is taken from the official proceedings; the two October arXiv manuscripts remain preprints. Other direct comparisons retain their previously documented verification scope.

No claim of algorithm originality follows from a title/abstract comparison. Relevant method descriptions were inspected; complete independent reproduction and systematic comparison with all stronger baselines are outstanding. The source URLs and versions identify what was inspected; none of the cited papers is claimed to validate PersonaSLM's particular annotation choices.

These observations should not be promoted to Level 2 replicated findings, Level 3 identified limitations of existing methods, or Level 4 independently validated algorithms. Negative baseline results and unstable/zero package averages are retained. No result is selected for statistical significance.

## Algorithmic Opportunity Assessment

**Observed Failure Modes:** boundary ambiguity and wrong-entity/time answers are inspectable; a history-induced internal selection mechanism is not identified. **Existing Methods and Published Prior Art:** admission, time filtering, retrieval, steering, verification and guided decoding cover substantial proposed territory. **Potential Algorithmic Improvements:** confidence-aware boundary abstention or joint entity/time verification remain incremental candidates, not demonstrated novelty. **Validation Feasibility:** independent measurement and fair baselines precede a new method claim; no new model calls were made. **Publication Value:** rigorous measurement robustness and independent replication can support an empirical paper even if every proposed algorithm matches a simpler existing method.

The first target remains PLOS ONE. Current development evidence does not establish submission readiness. Prioritize the independent annotation/replication proposal over expanding generation counts or renaming known procedures.
