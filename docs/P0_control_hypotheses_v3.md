# P0 Control Identification and Falsifiable Research Proposals

**PROPOSED ONLY.** Protocol v1.1, paper title and official contrasts remain unchanged. No hypothesis below has been tested by this tokenization/review-preparation work. These proposals preserve priorities A–E: S/F/H identification, history decomposition, structural versus semantic failure, model/post-training sensitivity and authorized English real-speaker validation. All effect directions remain empirical questions.

## Direct overlap and novelty limits

The [existing novelty matrix](related_work_novelty_matrix.md) already documents persona consistency, personalization, context interference, temporal memory and real-person simulation. W10 (GSM-IC) covers irrelevant context; W11 (ContextEcho) supplies persona-related length/structure controls; W12 (PRISM) compares role and prompt choices. These precedents mean token matching and generic persona/factuality comparisons cannot themselves establish originality.

Four especially relevant sources were inspected again in this review:

- **Principled Personas: Defining and Measuring the Intended Effects of Persona Prompting on Task Performance** (Luz de Araujo, Röttger, Hovy and Roth; EMNLP 2025) directly tests robustness to irrelevant persona attributes. Its experimental setup and result labels include Gemma-2-2B, Llama-3.2-3B and Qwen2.5-3B. Small-model testing is already covered. [Primary paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1364.pdf).
- **One Persona, Many Cues, Different Results: How Sociodemographic Cues Impact LLM Personalization** (Weeber, Neplenbroek, Batzner and Padó; ACL 2026) compares persona cues and cautions that cue choice can change conclusions. Preserving a wrapper and checking alternatives are methodological precautions, not unique contributions. [Primary record](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.2079/).
- **When Personalization Misleads: Understanding and Mitigating Hallucinations in Personalized LLMs** (Sun et al.; Findings of ACL 2026) explicitly addresses user-history-aligned factual distortions through PFQABench/FPPS. A generic claim that personalized history can harm factual reliability is occupied. [Primary record](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.395/).
- **TwinVoice: A Multi-dimensional Benchmark Towards Digital Twins via LLM Persona Simulation** (Bangde Du, Minghao Guo, Songming He, Ziyi Ye, Xi Zhu, Weihang Su, Shuqi Zhu, Yujia Zhou, Yongfeng Zhang, Qingyao Ai and Yiqun Liu; Findings of ACL 2026) separates social/interpersonal/narrative persona settings and six capabilities, including memory and linguistic fidelity. Its abstract reports limitations in style and recall. Authentic-person simulation and separate fidelity facets are not new. [Primary record](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.981/).

The first paper's relevant PDF sections were checked; the other three checks cover bibliographic records/abstracts. No datasets were downloaded, private conversations accessed, source repositories reproduced or mitigation training performed. The historical external audit's broader claims are not silently promoted to independently verified details. This is not an exhaustive literature search or priority certification.

## Five falsifiable proposals

Each requires a predeclared smallest effect of interest, eligible-case denominator, uncertainty method, multiplicity handling and untouched confirmation material after the appropriate research approvals. A nonsignificant result alone is not falsification; sufficient precision or a declared equivalence criterion is required. No numerical threshold or power claim is invented here.

| Priority / hypothesis | Required comparison | Supporting observation | Falsifying observation | Confounds, cost and novelty risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Style and history interact under shared evidence rather than only adding independent effects | Approved C0–C7 pilot with distinct, counterbalanced S/F/H realizations. For F=0, inspect the interaction `(C5−C3)−(C1−C0)`; distinguish it from average factorial main effects | A reproducible interaction exceeding the declared bound across separate prompt realizations | Interaction lies inside a sufficiently precise equivalence interval while manipulation checks pass | Shared wording, F/H information overlap, persona clustering and missing/unverifiable units. Core eight conditions plus controls; high novelty risk unless a stable interaction has explanatory value |
| B. Named historical answer competition explains part of an apparent history effect beyond generic context exposure | Separately approved relevance × time-validity × presentation manipulation with fact-equated prose/dialogue and speech-act-balanced content controls; A/B/C are preliminary diagnostics, not this design | Sensitivity tracks named relevant old facts after presentation/length/pragmatics are controlled | Precise absence of a relevant-name effect, or the effect disappears once pragmatics are balanced | Vague replies, clarification priming, date wording, token identities and semantic distance. High extra annotation/cell cost; strong prior overlap with factual personalization and memory studies |
| C. Some apparent reliability differences reflect response-unit failures rather than factual contradictions | Preserve frozen `response_status_v1` and `separate_axes_policy_v1`; annotate units independently; jointly report structural-status probabilities and factual support with all eligible-case denominators | Full-transcript versus valid first-unit conclusions differ systematically, with independently confirmed cross-turn material explaining discrepancies | Both unit definitions yield equivalent results with adequate annotation agreement and coverage | Conditioning on COMPLETE can introduce selection bias; do not silently drop ambiguous/truncated cases. Primarily reannotation cost, not mandatory new generation; parser sensitivity alone has limited novelty |
| D. Post-training changes the distribution of structural and semantic failure modes beyond model size alone | Matched Base/post-trained pairs where available, multiple sizes/families, correct templates, separately validated token budgets and same evidence/tasks | Replicated status-by-training interaction with residual differences beyond size and template sensitivity | Equivalent failure distributions within precision, or all apparent differences explained by template/budget choices | Training corpus, architecture, quantization and template differ; cross-model comparisons are not randomized training effects. Several model × condition cells; size-only novelty is already contradicted by prior work |
| E. A synthetic interaction boundary predicts a corresponding boundary in authorized English speaker data | Later approved external validation on temporally separated, authorized native-English conversations; separate fidelity and reliability labels, retained language/provenance metadata | Preregistered directional/rank or bounded-effect prediction transfers across held-out speakers and settings | A sufficiently powered reversal or near-zero effect contradicts the preregistered transfer prediction | Domain shift, author permissions, fact verification, selection and privacy. Highest data/annotation burden; existing real-person benchmarks make general simulation novelty weak |

These are inferential opportunities, not proposed new persona algorithms. A credible methodological contribution would need independent evidence that the control decomposition changes or stabilizes valid inferences across realizations and settings. Negative results and failure boundaries are informative. The present six development scenarios and24 exact matches supply no estimate of any persona effect, interaction, model-size trend or external generalization.

The [identification matrix](P0_control_identification_matrix_v3.md) limits the current controls' claims. Human semantic validation remains OPEN. None of these future hypotheses is required to be completed during P0, and no P1 experiment is authorized by this document.
