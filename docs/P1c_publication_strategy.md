# P1c Publication Strategy

Maintainer: Yaxin ZHENG. First submission target: PLOS ONE. The official paper title and formal Protocol v1.1 remain unchanged. This prospective package does not establish publication readiness.

## Evidence needed for the intended empirical submission

PLOS ONE's [publication criteria](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/criteria-for-publication) require original research, technically sound methods and analyses, supported conclusions, research integrity and appropriate reporting/data availability; valid negative and null results are eligible. Acceptance is not assured. The journal does not require this project to invent an algorithm or obtain positive persona effects.

| Requirement for our intended claim | Available evidence | Remaining work |
|---|---|---|
| Reproducible empirical foundation | Preserved P0/P1/P1b artifacts and researcher-confirmed development labels | Portable release inventory, licenses and independent auditability of local raw logs |
| Identifiable history comparisons | Prospective explicit R/T, no-history and neutral definitions | Researcher selection of narrow estimand, semantic approval and exact matching of final prompts |
| Meaningful outcome measurement | Separate factual, temporal, relevance/coherence, delivery and structure | Independent annotation reliability and response-unit validation; explicit unknown bounds |
| Adequate precision | Speaker-aware planning sensitivity | Approve useful interval widths, realistic assumptions and independent sample allocation |
| Independent evidence | Development banks only | Later frozen independent speakers/templates and full analysis specification; no tuned test reuse |
| Defensible contribution | Conditional-history and measurement hypotheses | Results that distinguish alternative explanations, plus careful direct-prior-work positioning |
| Ethical scope | Fictional English records only | Authorized naturalistic data and separate fidelity evaluation only if real-speaker claims are made |

An empirical synthetic study can make appropriately limited claims without immediately completing every future phase. It cannot claim real-speaker or cross-model generalization that has not been tested. External validation remains a long-term research priority; it is not retroactively a new P0 requirement.

## Recommended sequence

First resolve the scientific choice between the auxiliary R/T cross and a narrower direct stale-target comparison. Then review stimuli and matched control semantics and freeze an authorized development manifest. Use the resulting bounded study to assess manipulation and measurement feasibility, not to select favorable outcomes. Choose an independent validation allocation based on approved precision and coverage, not the significance of a pilot. Freeze new validation material and analysis before inference. Consider a separately versioned Instruct arm when it can resolve configuration dependence.

Do not expand merely to increase generations. If neutral semantics fail, if R does not reflect the intended question, or if the endpoint is not reliably measurable, additional calls do not repair the problem. A null result with informative uncertainty and a valid intervention can be useful. More errors do not by themselves prove a better benchmark.

## Optional extensions

Algorithm development is optional and currently unjustified. Presentation manipulation is secondary because it overlaps direct prior art. Large model sweeps, training, broad memory retrieval and all future phases are not prerequisites for this prospective packet. Higher-tier ambitions may motivate stronger theoretical or methodological novelty, but must not inflate the present experiment or redefine its estimand. The evidence required for each eventual claim should determine scope.
