# P1 Wave 1 Next-Experiment Recommendation

Maintainer: Yaxin ZHENG. Protocol v1.1 is the formal baseline. This recommendation incorporates the completed researcher-confirmed annotations and supersedes the earlier *planning recommendation* to obtain those missing labels; it does not rewrite the earlier audit or authorize another run.

## Recommended next step

**Choose D: develop independently specified, more discriminative evidence-grounded tasks and calibrate their evaluation before any further inference.** Seek researcher approval for that development scope and the later bounded feasibility run. Preserve easy positive-control items so that difficulty is measured rather than manufactured. Do not select new material by whether it makes persona conditions look worse.

The reason is empirical: all 72 factual and 48 temporal eligible outputs are Correct, all nine factual-task speakers score 5 on relevance/coherence in every condition, and all observed score variation comes from three nonfactual speakers. Expanding or changing models on the same narrow tasks may replicate ceilings without answering S/F/H reliability questions. A separate Instruct arm (B) can later test configuration robustness on the same independently frozen improved material. If the researcher instead prioritizes a response-structure question, C is a defensible narrow alternative, without claiming it resolves factual discrimination.

## Why a factual ceiling is plausible

Current factual tasks place the answer directly in a short trusted record, repeat a small set of city/membership predicates and ask mostly single-fact extraction. Temporal tasks often require selecting an explicitly current residence rather than integrating multiple event intervals. Histories are genuine dated past facts, not necessarily difficult contradictions of current evidence. F introduces unrelated supplementary facts. These properties can reduce discrimination; the current data do not causally isolate which property caused the ceiling.

Possible **future** material could require combining two supported facts, resolving carefully controlled references, comparing explicit valid-time intervals, or distinguishing source-supported statements from plausible but unsupported ones. Every task must remain answerable from the same Track A evidence in every condition. Difficulty levels should vary one demand at a time, with independent templates and speaker assignments. Keep answer information out of supplementary persona material. Do not withhold the answer to manufacture a failure, turn valid past facts into contradictions without declaring it, or migrate implicitly into Track B.

Before running such material, independently validate expected answers, metric applicability and interpretation of abstention/uncertainty; calibrate ordinal relevance/coherence anchors without optimizing against the old outputs. Retain old Wave 1 questions and all scores. New tasks are development material until a later authorized confirmatory freeze; no final test set is created here.

## Neutral-control gate and minimal next decisions

The existing [24-item control packet](validation/p1/control_review_v1.csv), [master](validation/p1/control_review_master_v1.json) and [guide](P1_annotation_guide.md) remain available unchanged. Human review is **0/24, OPEN**; tokenizer equality is not semantic approval. No C1−N_style or C3−N_history outcome is reported. Any new task set requires its own validated control applicability and review; old approvals cannot be transferred automatically.

The researcher should choose the next scientific question and material scope, not a favorable expected result. The minimum methodological work depends on that choice: approve independent stimuli/scoring and a bounded manifest for D; approve a versioned model/template for B; or approve a specified structural intervention for C. For control-adjusted claims, complete the 24 control reviews or review new task-specific controls before use. Independent rater validation and a sample/precision plan are necessary for stronger publication claims, but no artificial P0 reopening or broad indefinite remediation cycle is proposed.

The [factorial analysis](P1_wave1_exploratory_factorial_analysis.md) and [publication assessment](P1_wave1_publication_readiness.md) retain all zero, small, opposing and uncertain estimates. No present result warrants calling persona conditioning harmful, beneficial, absent in effect, or mechanistically understood in the population.
