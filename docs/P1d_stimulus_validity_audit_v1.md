# P1d stimulus validity audit — v1

Maintainer: Yaxin ZHENG. Candidate: independent_candidates_v4. This is an AI-assisted scientific proposal, not completed human validation or a confirmatory freeze.

The exhaustive census covers 288 cases, 144 synthetic persona identities, 1,152 active history texts and all 1,728 assembled Base prompts. Every required component was independently reconstructed through inclusive-date ledger lookups. No nonunique reference, false historical source, inconsistent entity routing, condition-dependent ground truth or malformed requested date was found. The [288-item review](validation/p1d/scientific_validation_v1/case_scientific_review_v1.csv) includes the actual question, evidence, derivation, temporal interpretation, limitations and blank researcher fields.

These dispositions combine inspection of the finite question/control grammars with exhaustive case-specific checks. They are not 288 independent human judgments. A case can be answerable while its template population and mechanism interpretation remain limited.

There are 72 cases per family and per domain, 144 requests for each requested-date stratum, and two different task families per speaker. The four families share a service-ledger construction: the domains are not four independently sampled applications. “Current” means the latest listed interval, queried on 2054-11-15; the evaluation date is 2054-12-31. Historical queries request 2054-05-15. Both dates must be scored literally. No weekday assertion is required.

E1V1 repeats the entire requested fact in 72 single-fact cases, one of two components in 144 multi-component/follow-up cases, and the entry fact of a chain in 72 relational cases. This is the declared answer-supporting positive control. It must not be described as non-answer topical history. The relational facts are adjacent within ledger blocks; reading a block can yield both outputs without demonstrating sequential reasoning. Follow-up history can supply a referent cue also supplied by fixed context. Neither task complexity nor necessity of each reasoning step is empirically established.

Persona profiles supply identity and a narrow style target, but no assignment/window answer values. Each speaker's two ledgers are explicitly separate fictional scenarios. This avoids a cross-case factual contradiction but does not simulate a globally coherent real biography. Keep these restrictions in empirical reporting. No stimulus bytes were corrected or replaced in this audit. [Machine-readable summary](validation/p1d/scientific_validation_v1/scientific_audit_summary_v1.json).

The [offline context-removal diagnostic](validation/p1d/scientific_validation_v1/context_removal_material_diagnostic_v1.json) covers all 72 follow-ups: the question omits the target name, and both named people have distinct ledger-supported answer pairs for the requested date. Fixed context resolves that ambiguity. This material check is not a new experimental arm and does not demonstrate that a model will use context rather than correlated persona/history cues.
