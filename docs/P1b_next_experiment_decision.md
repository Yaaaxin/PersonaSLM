## Precision gate

For a future speaker-level mean paired difference, the planning approximation is half-width `h = 1.96*s/sqrt(K)`, where K is independent speakers and s is the standard deviation of speaker-level contrasts. At h=0.10, hypothetical s=0.25 or 0.50 gives approximately 25 or 97 speakers. These are sensitivity assumptions, not measured population variances or a universal sample-size prescription.

Interactions can have larger ranges and variance. Structural missingness, unequal family coverage, annotation uncertainty and multiplicity can increase requirements. Twelve development clusters cannot justify narrow confirmatory intervals. Choose meaningful precision, endpoint hierarchy, independent sampling and multiplicity handling before a confirmatory freeze.
