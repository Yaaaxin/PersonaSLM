Maintainer: Yaxin ZHENG. This supplements, rather than rewrites, the [historical matrix](related_work_novelty_matrix.md). Primary-source metadata and targeted methods sections were checked through live search. No implementation below was reproduced in this literature check. Full statistical/appendix verification remains incomplete where stated. Conference-looking preprint headers do not establish acceptance.

## Search scope and screening

Searches included the exact seven requested paper titles, followed by `temporal memory admission retrieval evidence conflict benchmark algorithm 2026 arxiv`. Primary arXiv, ACL Anthology and ICLR proceedings sources were retained. Search results from blogs, social media and aggregators were used only to locate primary sources. This is targeted novelty monitoring, not a systematic-review completeness claim. New direct competitors found by the expanded search are MemConflict and STALE/CUPMem. Further retrieval/steering candidates require primary-method review before reuse.

## New evidence still required

The current P1c intervention is an auxiliary topic/date/value package, not an answer-conflict intervention. Its outcome cannot establish novelty over studies manipulating genuinely competing answers, retrieval admission or model representations. A useful falsification would show whether apparent temporal-package differences persist with independently counterbalanced values and query dates, stable answer-bearing evidence and measured structural coverage. A null result remains informative within that scope.

No prospective method currently qualifies as an independently validated original contribution. The separate literal-component baseline is known engineering and exposes measurement errors rather than improving generated answers. The [algorithm opportunity registry](validation/research_monitor_v1/algorithm_opportunity_registry_v1.json) tracks concrete baseline requirements and rejected claims. A new method must improve on applicable published baselines without simply deleting persona/history utility or receiving extra unaccounted compute.

For PLOS ONE, these checks support a credible empirical study but do not establish submission readiness. Independent data, reliable annotation, meaningful precision and transparent provenance remain necessary project work. No claim of assured acceptance is made.
