# Research Protocol v1.1 — English Language Scope Addendum

Date: 2026-10-12. Status: researcher-required language scope; effective prospectively from this addendum. The parent protocol remains v1.1.

Paper: **Understanding the Effects of Persona Conditioning on Dialogue Reliability in Small Language Models**.

English is the primary experimental language of PersonaSLM. This requirement covers synthetic persona conversations, authorized real-speaker datasets, prompts and intended responses, linguistic-style analysis, factual and temporal evaluation, conversational-coherence evaluation, human annotation materials, and scientific analysis and publication-ready reports.

Primary benchmark conversations must originate in English. Non-English conversations must not be silently translated and represented as native English persona data. Record original language, declared experimental language, data origin, source identifier/hash, and whether translation occurred. Any future translated auxiliary material requires explicit provenance and separation from the primary English benchmark; this addendum does not authorize its collection or use.

Preserve raw model outputs exactly, including unexpected non-English text. Record language deviations as observations; do not translate, filter, discard or regenerate responses to manufacture compliance. A declaration of English as the intended output language is not proof that every generated output is English.

Do not retroactively edit completed P0 datasets, prompts, outputs, manifests, scores or annotation records. Previously missing language metadata may be documented in a dated sidecar with its evidence and retrospective status. Historical Chinese-language engineering documentation may remain as historical records; new primary annotation and scientific diagnostic reports are written in English. Administrative conversation with the researcher may use Chinese.

All research objectives, S/F/H factors, Track A/B definitions, frozen evidence, evaluation procedures, applicability decisions and phase boundaries remain unchanged. This is a language-scope addendum, not a new treatment or change to completed experiments. P1, LoRA and access to MimooPet remain outside the current authorization. Commits and pushes remain exclusively the researcher's responsibility.
