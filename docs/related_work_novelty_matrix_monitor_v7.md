# Related work and novelty monitor v7

Maintainer: Yaxin ZHENG. Focused AI-assisted update on 2026-10-14; retain v3–v6 and their source lineage. Primary methods/results were inspected, not merely titles. Implementations were not executed unless the local baseline record explicitly says so. No new algorithmic originality is verified.

| [Rasmussen, Paliychuk, Beauvais, Ryan and Chalef, Zep: A Temporal Knowledge Graph Architecture for Agent Memory](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.13956v1), arXiv:2501.13956v1, 2025 preprint; peer review not verified | Episode/entity/community graph, source links, world-valid and transaction times, entity/fact deduplication and contradiction invalidation. Uses model-based graph construction and hybrid retrieval/reranking; evaluates dialogue memory and LongMemEval. Direct precedent for temporal evidence-state provenance. | ESCP's typed state is not new. A CPU adaptation must count extraction/index costs and compare equivalent query/evidence budgets. Reject originality if the same graph/rule operations explain the proposed benefit. |
| [Chhikara, Khant, Aryan, Singh and Yadav, Mem0: Building Production-Ready AI Agents with Scalable Long-Term Memory](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.19413v1), arXiv:2504.19413v1, 2025 preprint; peer review not verified | Extract memories from dialogue with summary/recent context; choose add/update/delete/no-op against retrieved memories. Graph variant adds typed relations and conflict handling. LOCOMO-based evaluation includes quality and resource costs. Uses larger model services/embedding infrastructure than the current local checker. | Updating an evidence store or filtering obsolete facts is established. Compare errors caused by extraction, invalidation and retrieval separately. A supplied-metadata local rule is a privileged diagnostic, not a faithful Mem0 replication. |
| [Liu and Ding, What to Admit and How to Present](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.11188v1), 2026 preprint | FC/PS adjudicate situated factors or typed units into permitted channels; shared admission decisions distinguish selection from PN/CH presentation. Matched random/relevance admission controls and beneficial-personalization evaluation make this especially close to ESCP. External adjudication has nontrivial cost. | A typed gate preserving persona content already overlaps substantially. Need an explicit computational difference plus matched-information, fidelity and total-cost comparison. A prospective venue heading is not proof of acceptance. |
| [When Personalization Misleads / FPPS](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.395.pdf), Findings ACL 2026, DOI10.18653/v1/2026.findings-acl.395 | Layer selection, trained probing and hard/soft/mixed activation steering distinguish factuality and personalization. Backbones include 7B–14B instruction models. | Priority prompting is not FPPS. Local faithful testing needs activation access and probe data, independently held out; no evidence that FPPS fails our CPU cases exists. |
| [LongMemEval](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.10813v2), ICLR 2025 | Time-aware query expansion, fact-augmented indexing and readers separate retrieval and evidence use. Smaller temporal extraction can fail. | Current supplied-date filters already succeed on curated pools. Free-text temporal extraction, faithful cost accounting and fixed-evidence reader failures remain untested differentiators. |
| [Chain-of-Verification](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.212.pdf), Findings ACL 2024; [RARR](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.910.pdf), ACL 2023 | CoVe drafts, plans independent verification questions and revises; RARR researches evidence and edits while preserving the original response. Both need additional computation. | A component checker is a simplified engineering baseline. Compare full compute budgets and style preservation; do not assert these published methods are inadequate without testing. |
| [TwinVoice](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.981.pdf), Du et al., Findings ACL 2026 | Discriminative choices and generative ranking/scoring across social/interpersonal/narrative personas; lexical, tonal and syntactic fidelity. Human/judge checks and alternative judges are reported. | Separate quality/fidelity panels in PersonaSLM address a specific controlled measurement need, not a novel fidelity construct. | [Persistent Personas?](https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-long.246.pdf), de Araujo et al., EACL 2026 | Persona-directed and goal-oriented long dialogues, prefix conditioning, multiple model families, role/BFI/instruction and style evaluation. Persona drift and task/fidelity tradeoffs already studied. | Fixed sufficient evidence and controlled S/F/H manipulations may offer complementary evidence, but no claim that history-related fidelity degradation itself is new. |
| [Decoupling Logic from Persona](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.09772v2), Nakatsu and Wang, 2026 preprint | AO-DA separates processing paths with micro-state, routing and adapter configurations; raw/structured/spoken metrics and pollution ablations. | Two-stage separation and structural/semantic divergence are prior art. Constant clean-path inputs, model/adapter differences and narrow tasks limit equivalence; independent matched-evidence testing is needed. |
| [Wiki-Talkie](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.08513v1), Fucci et al., 2026 preprint | Natural Wikipedia discussion personas with profile/behavior/history variants; multilingual lexical/semantic and dialogue-behavior comparisons. | Population discourse similarity is different from individual fidelity and fixed-evidence task reliability. No data are imported; permission and independent naturalistic validation remain P4 requirements. |

Methods sections of TwinVoice and Persistent Personas? were revisited for the new fidelity procedure. Their reported agreement does not supply agreement for PersonaSLM. The pre-existing published grammar, constrained decoding and native-boundary comparisons in v5 remain applicable; EOS-name compatibility is an engineering correction, not a new boundary algorithm.

The new eight-request H_none feasibility data cannot test S/F/H interference. The ESCP scaffold ties a simple declarative checker on all 16 authored fixtures. Both findings narrow the presently defensible claim: controlled empirical measurement under persona conditioning is the main contribution path; an original algorithm remains an unverified possibility contingent on a residual failure beyond strong baselines.
