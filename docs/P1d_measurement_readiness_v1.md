# P1d measurement readiness — v1

Maintainer: Yaxin ZHENG. No new model responses, human ratings, reviewer appointments or agreement statistics exist.

The [applicability check](validation/p1d/scientific_validation_v1/metric_applicability_verification_v1.json) confirms all nine registered metrics on 1,728 prospective Base outputs, with 288 expected in the matched Instruct arm. Eligibility is separate from assessability. Missing, evidence-unverifiable, interpretation-uncertain, boundary-unresolved, structurally unscorable and N/A states remain distinct. Factual Incorrect incidence is not assessed-only accuracy; task success requires supported complete delivery.

`tools/p1d_metric_contracts.py` supplies separate evidence-input contracts for factual claims, temporal judgments, task/component delivery and ordinal reviews. It cannot score free text, create human evidence or prove psychometric independence. Eight authored software fixtures demonstrate allowed separations: correct facts with partial/no answer; wrong entity with unresolved temporal interpretation; delivered but unsupported answer; unresolved boundary. They are not model outputs or validation accuracy. The earlier development checker returned task_success=false alongside its abstention flag; this must not be imported as an official Failure. The new contract retains Uncertain explicitly. Historical annotations and the approved rubric are untouched.

Separate relevance and coherence passes require their own quotes, reasons, rubric anchors, alternatives and uncertainty. Persona Fidelity uses the frozen narrow style target, not correctness. Do not force score variation: report ceilings, concordance and zero variance. P1c showed a coherence ceiling and factual/task equality; the new two-component cases allow discrimination, but do not guarantee it. Single-fact positive controls remain especially weak for separating factual correctness from task success.

The [metric review contract](validation/p1d/scientific_validation_v1/metric_review_contract_v1.json) supplements the adopted annotation plan without changing endpoints. The existing 72-item masked P1c boundary package is available for an actually independent reviewer. Future P1d sampling remains 36 prospectively selected speaker clusters (432 Base outputs and matched Instruct outputs where present), with independent human passes before AI suggestions and separate adjudication. Boundary review preserves multiple defensible spans and the raw continuation. No original parser is a gold standard.

The independent reviewer is not yet available in the records. Readiness targets—90% categorical agreement, no unresolved systematic rubric conflict and at most 5% unresolved boundaries in a measurement pilot—have not been demonstrated. No confirmatory measurement PASS is issued. If independent review cannot be obtained, its consequence for the approved validation claim must be explicitly resolved, not hidden by adopting AI scores twice.
