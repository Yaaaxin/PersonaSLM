# P1d precision and dependence — v1

Maintainer: Yaxin ZHENG. All new numerical results here are synthetic simulations, not P1d model outcomes or empirical ICC estimates.

The approved 144-speaker/288-case allocation and six primary endpoint-by-contrast comparisons remain unchanged. The previous 216-scenario grid and stress results remain valid under their stated assumptions. This additional [108-scenario simulation](validation/p1d/scientific_validation_v1/template_precision_simulation_v1.json) uses 128 replications each (13,824 trials, 324 summaries), seed 716702 plus scenario index. It varies hypothetical N=96/144/192, prevalence .25/.50/.75, template-slope SD 0/.05/.10, absent versus E-dependent missingness (.20/.05), and E effects 0/.05. N=192 is planning sensitivity, not an expanded approved cohort.

Four common template-ancestor slopes affect E, V and E×V; balanced family prevalence offsets, speaker baseline shifts and paired-cell uniforms supply additional dependence. These are hypothetical parameters, not measured correlations. Probability clipping is explicit. Null superpopulation coverage is reported only in symmetric zero-effect scenarios. Complete-data intervals use latent simulated outcomes and are optimistic under missingness; no missing real outcome is imputed.

At N=144, prevalence .5, no missingness and template-slope SD .10, median six-comparison half-widths are about **6.88 pp E, 6.91 pp V and 13.48 pp E×V**. Ordinary 95% interval coverage for the sampled fixed-template target is .953/.938/.945. For a hypothetical new-template null target it is only **.602/.609/.813**. These are 128-replication Monte Carlo proportions, with appreciable simulation error, not future coverage guarantees. Their difference shows why adding speakers cannot replace independent template replication. The exact approved speaker-only estimator is not silently replaced by a new template estimator.

With condition-dependent missingness, unknown-outcome identification widths approach **25 pp main / 50 pp interaction**. These are identification regions, not confidence intervals. More N does not remove them. Interactions' ±1 weights amplify uncertainty relative to averaged-main ±.5 weights. Retain all categories, original signed weights, whole-speaker bootstrap sensitivity, leave-one-speaker-out, task/date strata and selected-subset sensitivity; never renormalize surviving cells.

The proposed practical thresholds remain 10 pp main and 20 pp interaction. The current design does not promise useful precision for smaller effects or equivalence. The 24-speaker Instruct subset is secondary and substantially less precise. Resolve the target population and template dependence before confirmatory freeze; if the intended claim requires new-template generalization, the current template construction and speaker-only precision justification are insufficient.
