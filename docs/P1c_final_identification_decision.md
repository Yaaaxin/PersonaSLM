# P1c Final Identification Decision — v1

Maintainer: Yaxin ZHENG. Status: AI-assisted proposal awaiting researcher approval. No P1c output has been generated.

## Verdict

**The six original cells support bounded, operational package comparisons. They do not isolate pure relevance or temporal mechanisms.** All 12 cases are proposed as ACCEPT_WITH_RESTRICTED_ESTIMAND; none requires reference-answer repair. The [case and text decisions](P1c_final_researcher_review_packet.md) state the remaining semantic decisions explicitly.

R and T can be crossed operationally: each case has R0T0, R0T1, R1T0 and R1T1, plus N_history and H_none. They are not independently varied latent constructs. R replaces music-label with parcel-label propositions and lexical/color cues. T replaces an expired interval/value record with one valid at the requested date. Entity attribution is **constant**, not a new competing speaker: both topics concern the same fictional speaker. The changing entity-like cues are attribute/domain words and values, not target identity. No pure mechanism follows from mathematical factorial balance.

## Evidence and counterfactual comparison

The [exhaustive field/confound inventory](validation/p1c/final_review_v1/intervention_confound_inventory_v1.json) preserves every original selected-record field comparison and all 72 unordered pairs of R/T cells. Across six cells, the evidence, question, context, evaluation/requested dates, reference components, applicability and model settings remain fixed. Only the selected condition, historical text/source association, resulting prompt/hash/token sequence and history-dependent context offsets change.

Across the 24 T-only pairs, predicate and speaker remain fixed but color and interval change. Across the 24 R-only pairs, interval and speaker remain fixed but predicate and color change. The remaining 24 pairs change both factors. All have equal prompt lengths, archive headers, speaker roles and turn counts; none retains an identical entity-predicate-value proposition across the pair. Twelve cross-topic pairs reuse a color, reflecting the small cyclic assignment scheme rather than independent lexical counterbalancing.

The source solver returns the same answer after deleting or substituting all parcel/music facts. Deleting a required target fact removes the unique reference derivation. Direct target answers are absent from all 48 active-history texts. These are reference-model checks, not proof that an actual language model cannot exploit patterns, or that auxiliary labels have no psychological salience.

## Three separate concepts

| Concept | Current implementation | Valid claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Topical relevance | Parcel-label versus music-label auxiliary content | Explicitly related to parcel-route questions; a plausible but implicit relation to collection desk/day questions requires review |
| Temporal status | True record expired versus valid at the requested date | Interval/value package effect; a historical query may correctly select an older fact |
| Answer competition | Not directly manipulated: labels do not name the requested desk/day/room | No estimate of stale target-answer competition |

Both auxiliary records already exist in the shared trusted ledger. Additional history repeats and highlights one record; it does not change answer-relevant evidence availability. R/T effects may reflect repetition, lexical associations or salience. Task family is nested within pairs of speakers, so between-family patterns cannot uniquely establish task-demand or relevance mechanisms.

Historical queries request a date before the archive recording date. They ask retrospectively about scheduled truth, not what a speaker could have known at that earlier time. Future intervals appear as scheduled records. This interpretation is logically coherent and preserves the references, but must be explicitly accepted rather than mislabeled as time-constrained memory retrieval.

## Supported and prohibited contrasts

- Fixed-factor simple contrasts: differences between named auxiliary content packages at one level of the other factor; easier to interpret than a latent-mechanism claim.
- Average R/T: equally weighted averages of those replacements within each case. Averaging does not remove their lexical coupling.
- R×T: operational difference in differences. It cannot establish a unique interaction between semantic relevance and temporal reasoning.
- Active versus N_history: length/turn/wrapper-controlled content replacement **if approved**; procedural priming and interval information differ.
- Active versus H_none: total added-history package, including 65 or 66 additional tokens and archive framing. Not length-controlled.

The [registry](P1c_final_estimand_registry.md) fixes all 16 weights and restrictions before any outcome exists. S=F=0 here; these effects are not the P1b S/F/H main or interaction effects.

## Separate prospective answer-competition note

A future independently approved design could contrast historical records of the **target answer predicate** that are valid versus expired at the requested date, alongside auxiliary-topic records. Common trusted evidence must still contain sufficient interval-resolved truth in every condition, with both current and historical questions. Independently counterbalanced entities/values, equal formatting/length and reviewed answer requirements would be needed. A newest-fact heuristic should fail some historical questions by design, without false ground truth. Such a design could distinguish direct candidate-answer exposure from topical cues; it is not present in this bank and is not added to the 72 requests.

## Decision boundary

Preserve the existing bank if the researcher accepts this narrow diagnostic as informative. If the intended scientific claim remains an isolated temporal or stale-answer mechanism, the current design is **NO-GO for that claim**; approval cannot make it identifiable. Limited development acceptance does not endorse every failed criterion as satisfied. No mandatory requirement for an algorithm, positive effect, perfect model termination or a larger sample is introduced.
