Methods and result sections were inspected, with scope recorded per entry. The ICML venue PDF fetch failed; [arXiv v2 methods and Appendix L](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.03305v2) were available. Exact venue/preprint byte equivalence remains unverified. The venue record spells one author Jaime S. Ide while arXiv says James S. Ide; both source forms are preserved rather than silently reconciled. These studies do not independently validate PersonaSLM's CPU configuration or persona-fidelity tradeoff.

The [LongMemEval implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xiaowu0162/LongMemEval/main/src/index_expansion/temp_query_search_pruning.py) provides an important implementation distinction: query-time extraction and temporal candidate reordering are not identical to the local supplied-date hard filter. The official code was read, not executed, and its movable branch was not treated as a pinned replication. [FPPS](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.395/) uses activation steering; the current priority-prompt baseline does not reproduce it. [CoVe](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.212/) and [RARR](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.910/) require verification/revision work beyond an offline structured checker. [Admission and presentation](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.11188v1) remain directly overlapping preprint methods.

The remaining defensible research opportunity is an independently measured reliability–delivery–fidelity–CPU comparison under fixed sufficient evidence and controlled persona packages. It is a prospective empirical contribution, not an established new mechanism. A new parser, date filter or checklist does not meet the threshold for original algorithmic contribution. Strong simple and published baselines, adverse outcomes and independent measurement must precede such a claim.
